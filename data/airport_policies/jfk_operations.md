# JFK Airport Operations Policy

## Policy ID
JFK-OPS-001

## Airport
John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)

## Driver Queue Rules

Drivers must enter the designated airport staging area before accepting airport pickup requests.

Drivers must remain in the staging queue until assignment.

Drivers leaving the staging queue lose their queue position.

## Pickup Rules

1. Drivers may collect passengers only from authorized pickup areas.
2. Drivers must not wait in restricted loading areas.
3. Drivers must follow airport traffic-control instructions.

## Operational Threshold

The expected completion rate is above 85%.

Completion rates below 80% require operational investigation.

## ETA

Average airport pickup ETA should normally remain below 15 minutes.

## Monitoring

Operations teams should monitor:

- Completion rate
- Average ETA
- Active drivers
- Driver cancellation rate
- Queue size
- Request volume

## Airport Restrictions

Drivers must comply with airport staging, traffic, pickup-zone, and security requirements.
