# SFO Driver Policy

## Policy ID
SFO-DRIVER-001

## Airport
San Francisco International Airport (SFO)

## Queue Position

Drivers must remain in the designated airport staging queue after entering the staging area.

Leaving the queue voluntarily causes the driver to lose queue priority.

Drivers must not bypass the designated queue process.

## Queue Abandonment

Repeated queue abandonment may result in temporary restrictions on airport pickup eligibility.

## Driver Incentives

Driver incentives may be considered when available driver supply is insufficient to meet airport demand.

Incentive recommendations should consider:

- Number of additional drivers required
- Operational severity
- Expected duration of the issue
- Estimated incentive cost

## Cancellation

A driver cancellation rate above 15% should trigger operational investigation.

A sustained increase in cancellation rate should be investigated together with queue size, request volume, and active-driver availability.

## Driver Conduct

Drivers must follow airport staging, pickup, traffic, and security procedures.

Failure to follow airport rules may result in operational restrictions.
