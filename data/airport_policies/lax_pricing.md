# LAX Pricing and Surge Policy

## Policy ID
LAX-PRICE-001

## Airport
Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)

## Standard Surge

The standard airport surge multiplier is 1.0x.

## Surge Conditions

Surge may be introduced when passenger demand materially exceeds available driver supply.

Factors considered during surge review include:

- Request volume
- Active-driver availability
- Completion rate
- Driver cancellation rate
- Passenger wait time

## Maximum Surge

The maximum permitted surge multiplier at LAX is 1.6x.

The system must block any request above 1.6x.

## Approval Requirement

A surge multiplier below 1.3x is medium risk.

A surge multiplier of 1.3x or higher requires human approval.

## Surge Override

Every override must include:

- Airport code
- New multiplier
- Reason
- Approval status

## Policy Violation

A recommendation above 1.6x must be rejected.

The AI must not bypass the maximum surge limit.
