# SFO Airport Operations Policy

## Policy ID
SFO-OPS-001

## Airport
San Francisco International Airport (SFO)

## Purpose
This policy defines operational rules for airport rideshare activity at SFO.

## Driver Queue Rules

1. Drivers must enter the designated airport staging area before accepting airport pickup requests.
2. Drivers must remain in the designated staging queue until a ride request is assigned.
3. Drivers who voluntarily leave the staging queue lose their queue position.
4. Drivers must not bypass other drivers in the designated queue.

## Pickup Rules

1. Passenger pickups must occur only in designated commercial pickup areas.
2. Drivers must not wait in active passenger loading lanes.
3. Drivers must follow airport traffic-control instructions.
4. Drivers must move away from the pickup zone after completing a passenger pickup.

## Operational Thresholds

The expected airport ride completion rate is above 85%.

A completion rate below 80% requires operational investigation.

A completion rate below 75% is classified as a high operational concern.

## ETA Threshold

The normal average airport pickup ETA should remain below 15 minutes.

An average ETA above 15 minutes should be investigated when accompanied by low completion or high cancellation rates.

## Queue Monitoring

The operations team should monitor:

- Queue size
- Active drivers
- Driver cancellation rate
- Completion rate
- Average ETA
- Request volume

## Airport Restrictions

Drivers must comply with airport security, traffic-control, staging, and pickup-area requirements.

## Policy Enforcement

Violations of airport operating procedures may result in temporary restrictions on airport pickup eligibility.
