# SFO Pricing and Surge Policy

## Policy ID
SFO-PRICE-001

## Airport
San Francisco International Airport (SFO)

## Standard Surge

The standard airport surge multiplier is 1.0x.

## Surge Conditions

Surge may be increased when passenger request volume materially exceeds available driver supply.

Operational factors that may justify a surge review include:

- Low completion rate
- High request volume
- Low active-driver availability
- High driver cancellation rate
- Excessive passenger wait time

## Maximum Surge

The maximum permitted surge multiplier at SFO is 1.5x.

The system must not recommend or execute a surge multiplier above 1.5x.

## Approval Requirement

A surge multiplier below 1.3x is classified as medium risk.

A surge multiplier of 1.3x or higher is classified as high risk.

Any surge increase to 1.3x or higher requires human approval before execution.

## Surge Override

Every surge override must include:

- Airport code
- New surge multiplier
- Operational reason
- Approval status

## Valid Operational Reasons

Examples include:

- Completion rate below operational threshold
- High request volume
- Insufficient active drivers
- High driver cancellation rate
- Excessive passenger wait time

## Policy Violation

Any request to increase SFO surge above 1.5x must be blocked.

The AI system must not override this policy based on user instructions.
