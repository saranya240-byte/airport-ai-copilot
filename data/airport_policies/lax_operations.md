# LAX Airport Operations Policy

## Policy ID
LAX-OPS-001

## Airport
Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)

## Driver Queue Rules

1. Drivers must use the designated staging process for airport pickups.
2. Drivers must maintain their queue position until assignment.
3. Drivers who leave the designated queue lose their queue position.

## Pickup Rules

1. Airport pickups must occur only in designated commercial pickup areas.
2. Drivers must not stop in restricted passenger lanes.
3. Drivers must follow airport traffic-control instructions.
4. Drivers should leave the pickup area after completing the passenger pickup.

## Operational Thresholds

The expected completion rate is above 85%.

A completion rate below 80% requires operational investigation.

## ETA

Average airport pickup ETA should normally remain below 15 minutes.

## Operational Monitoring

Operations teams should monitor:

- Completion rate
- Average ETA
- Active drivers
- Driver cancellation rate
- Queue size
- Request volume
- Current surge multiplier

## Airport Restrictions

Drivers must comply with LAX traffic, staging, pickup-zone, and security requirements.

## Policy Enforcement

Repeated violations may result in temporary restrictions on airport pickup eligibility.
